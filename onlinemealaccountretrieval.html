Key Features

Real-Time Balance Display

Account balances displayed reflect the MiChoice system in real time — the same balance the cafeteria POS sees at the serving line.

Transaction History

Parents can review recent transactions — which meals were purchased, what they cost, and when payments were applied. Account transparency without requiring a call.

Low-Balance Email Alerts

Automated email notifications when a student's balance falls below a parent-set threshold — giving families time to top up before the account reaches zero.

Multi-Student Households

Parents with multiple students can check all of their children's accounts in a single session — no need to look up each student separately.

No Staff Involvement Required

Self-service balance retrieval means parents get the information they need without involving cafeteria or district office staff — freeing staff for operational priorities.

Available Any Time

The parent portal is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. No waiting until the cafeteria opens. No phone tag with office staff.

Read-Only Access

Account retrieval is a view-only function — balances and history are visible, but no changes can be made. Payments are handled separately through mymealmoney.com.

Direct Path to Payment

When a parent checks the balance and sees it's low, the portal links directly to mymealmoney.com — the district's RevTrak-powered online payment portal — to add funds immediately.

Need to add funds? Online Meal Account Retrieval shows you the balance. To add money to a student's account, use the district's mymealmoney.com portal — secure online payments via credit or debit card, powered by RevTrak. Payments import automatically into MiChoice. No manual entry required.

The Balance Inquiry Problem

In school cafeterias, one of the most common sources of office interruption is the parent balance inquiry — "What's my child's account balance?" It seems simple, but fielding those calls requires a staff member to stop what they're doing, look up the student, and read the balance. Across a district, these inquiries add up to a significant volume of low-value interruptions to operational staff.

Online Meal Account Retrieval eliminates those calls. Parents check the balance themselves, online, at whatever hour is convenient for them. The cafeteria office doesn't get the call because the parent already has the answer.

Preventing Low-Balance Disruptions

The more disruptive scenario isn't the balance inquiry call — it's the student who arrives at the serving line with an empty or nearly empty account. Handling that situation takes serving line time, creates awkward moments for the student, and requires follow-up contact with the family. Low-balance email alerts address the problem before it reaches the cafeteria.

When a parent sets a low-balance threshold and receives an automatic alert, they have time to add funds before the account is depleted. The link from the alert — or from the balance retrieval portal — goes directly to mymealmoney.com, where they can add funds immediately. The payment imports automatically into MiChoice. By the next meal service, the account is funded.

Part of the Complete Online Services Suite

Online Meal Account Retrieval is one component of MiChoice's complete suite of online parent services. The Online F&R Application Portal handles eligibility applications digitally. mymealmoney.com handles online payments via RevTrak. Account Retrieval closes the loop by giving parents visibility into the account between application and payment — keeping families informed and engaged without creating work for district staff.

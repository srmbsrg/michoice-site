How eMeal Works

eMeal operates as a client/server Point of Sale program on any brand of Windows-based touch screen PC. The touch interface is designed for the realities of a cafeteria environment — gloved hands, fast transactions, minimal training required for serving line staff.

At the serving line, the system identifies the student (via biometric scan, PIN, or ID card), retrieves their eligibility status in real time from COMS-PRO, records the transaction, and updates account balances instantly. The entire process takes seconds per student.

Integration with the MiChoice Suite

eMeal is the front end of the MiChoice ecosystem. It connects in real time to COMS-PRO (Central Office Management) for eligibility status and district reporting, to FAR-Apps for free and reduced status updates, and to mymealmoney.com for balance pre-payments made by parents online.

When a parent pays online at 7pm, the balance is available at the serving line the next morning. When an eligibility determination changes, the new status is live at the POS within minutes — not days.

Reporting Capabilities

eMeal generates a comprehensive library of child nutrition management reports — daily meal counts by category, revenue reconciliation, participation rates by school or district, reimbursable meal tracking, and more. Everything your child nutrition director needs for USDA reporting and operational decision-making.

The Payment Collection Challenge

Collecting meal account payments from families has historically been a friction-heavy process — cash sent with students, checks processed manually, balances updated by hand. Each of these touchpoints creates administrative work, increases error risk, and creates cash handling security exposure.

Online meal payment solves all three. Parents pay digitally at their convenience. RevTrak handles the payment security. The daily automatic import into MiChoice eliminates manual processing. The result is less work for district staff, faster balance updates for students, and a more convenient experience for families.

The RevTrak Partnership

MiChoice's online payment solution is powered by RevTrak — the premier provider of credit and debit card solutions for K-12 schools, with over 20 years serving the education market. RevTrak's platform is PCI-certified, meaning cardholder data is protected to the payment industry's highest standards. Districts don't need to build or maintain their own payment security infrastructure — RevTrak handles it.

Fund Accounting and Reconciliation

Online payments are easy to receive but can create reconciliation complexity if the reporting isn't designed for it. RevTrak's fund accounting reports are built specifically for school finance offices — making the reconciliation between collected payments and account postings straightforward and audit-friendly.

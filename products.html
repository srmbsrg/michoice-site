miComs (COMS-PRO)

Central Office Management Software

The hub of district nutrition data.

COMS-PRO is the data backbone of every MiChoice deployment.   • District-wide WAN integration

  • Government reporting
  • Meal accountability tracking
  • Connects all MiChoice modules

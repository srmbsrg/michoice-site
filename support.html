Frequently Asked Questions

What are your support hours?

Our telephone help desk is available during school day hours. Emergency support for critical issues that affect active lunch service is available outside normal hours. Contact us for specific hours for your region.

How quickly do you respond to support calls?

Telephone support connects you with a live technician immediately during business hours. We do not use automated phone trees for support calls. You reach a person who knows MiChoice software.

Are software updates included in the support agreement?

Yes. Regulatory updates, bug fixes, and minor feature additions are delivered as part of your ongoing support agreement. Major version upgrades are discussed separately depending on scope.

What if we add new staff mid-year who need training?

Training is not a one-time event. MiChoice provides training and re-training throughout the relationship. Contact us to schedule on-site or remote training for new staff at any time.

Can you support a district that's migrating from a different system?

Yes. Data migration from legacy systems is a standard part of our implementation service. We have experience migrating from a wide range of legacy K-12 food service platforms.

HACCP Documentation: The Problem It Solves

HACCP compliance documentation is a daily obligation for school food service operations. Every temperature check, every receiving log, every corrective action must be recorded, organized, and retained for inspection. Done manually on paper, this represents thousands of data points per school per year — all of which must be findable on short notice when an inspector arrives.

Sentry9000 replaces that paper mountain with automated data collection and digital records. IoT sensors continuously record temperature data from every cold storage and hot holding unit. Digital forms replace paper logs. Incidents are flagged in real time rather than discovered retroactively. And when an audit happens, the response is measured in minutes, not hours.

The EZ-Task Integration

One of the most valuable features of the Sentry9000 / MiChoice integration is the seamless link between HACCP production records and EZ-Task inventory. When Sentry9000 records that a batch of a menu item was prepared, those quantities automatically flow into EZ-Task, depleting the corresponding ingredient items. The kitchen doesn't need to manually update two systems. The inventory is always current without the extra step.

The No-News-Is-Good-News Approach

Sentry9000 is designed around exception reporting. The system collects data continuously — but only surfaces items that require attention. If all sensors are reporting normal readings and all forms are completed within limits, staff see a clean dashboard. When something falls out of limits, the incident is immediately visible and actionable. This keeps the daily burden minimal while maintaining complete documentation.

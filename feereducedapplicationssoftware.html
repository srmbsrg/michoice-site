The F&R Processing Challenge

Free and reduced meal eligibility processing is one of the most labor-intensive administrative functions in school food service. Districts receive thousands of applications. Each must be reviewed, verified, determined, entered into the system, and communicated back to the family — with every step documented for potential audit.

Without automation, this process consumes enormous staff time and creates significant risk of error — both in the determinations themselves and in the updates to the cafeteria serving line. A student with a pending determination who is incorrectly charged full price creates both financial and compliance problems.

How FAR-Apps Solves It

FAR-Apps automates the entire workflow. Applications enter through the online portal or paper scanning process, flow through the determination logic, and update COMS-PRO and every cafeteria POS automatically. Staff manage exceptions and edge cases — the routine determinations happen without manual intervention.

The result is an immediate and measurable reduction in administrative labor costs, while simultaneously reducing determination errors and improving the speed with which eligible students receive their benefits.

Federal Funding Impact

Every properly processed F&R application translates directly to federal reimbursement. Districts that process applications accurately and completely capture every dollar they're entitled to. FAR-Apps assures your district of maximum school lunch funding while maintaining a complete compliance audit trail — so a state or federal audit doesn't become a financial risk.

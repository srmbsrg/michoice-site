The District Data Hub

COMS-PRO serves as the focal point for all information flow throughout the MiChoice ecosystem. As an all-inclusive back office product, it provides the district child nutrition department with a common data source for every function — from F&R processing to meal accountability to government reporting.

The system allows for bi-directional, real-time transfer of data between COMS-PRO and every MiChoice FAR-Apps and eMeal POS installation across the district's WAN. A status change at the central office is live at the serving line in minutes. A meal transaction at any school is visible at the central office immediately.

Centralized F&R Management

Free and reduced eligibility is one of the most administratively intensive functions in school food service. COMS-PRO centralizes the entire workflow — application intake (via FAR-Apps and the online portal), determination, status assignment, and POS update — so that district staff manage the process from one system instead of coordinating across multiple schools and applications.

Government Reporting Made Manageable

USDA and state reporting requirements are complex and change regularly. COMS-PRO generates all required reports directly from its centralized database — no manual data collection from individual schools, no spreadsheet assembly, no last-minute scrambles. Audit trails are built in from day one.

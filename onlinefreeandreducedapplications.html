Key Features

District-Branded Portal

Each district's online application portal is branded to match their identity — families see a familiar, trustworthy interface, not a generic third-party form.

Direct FAR-Apps Integration

Submitted applications flow directly into FAR-Apps for processing. No manual handoff, no re-entry — the digital submission is the intake record.

Multi-Student Households

A single application covers all students in the household. Families don't need to complete separate applications for each child.

Application Status Tracking

Families can check the status of their submitted application online — reducing status inquiry calls to district offices.

Automated Notifications

Determination letters — approval, denial, and verification requests — are generated and delivered automatically once FAR-Apps completes processing.

NSLP Compliance Built In

The application form captures every data element required by NSLP regulations — income documentation, household size, benefit certifications, and required disclosures.

Secure Submission

Applications are submitted over encrypted connections. Sensitive household income and benefit data is handled in compliance with applicable privacy requirements.

Replaces Paper Entirely

Districts can offer the online portal as the primary application channel — paper remains available for households without internet access, processed via F&R Scanning Software.

The Paper Application Problem

For decades, free and reduced meal applications meant paper — forms sent home in backpacks, returned crumpled and sometimes incomplete, collected at the school office, sorted, batched, and manually entered into the eligibility system. Every step in that chain is labor, every step introduces delay, and every step is an opportunity for an application to be lost, misread, or miskeyed.

The online application portal eliminates every one of those steps. The family completes the form at home on any device. The submission is instant, complete, and already in the format FAR-Apps needs. The district receives a clean digital record the moment the family submits.

Higher Participation, More Federal Funding

Districts consistently find that easier application processes lead to higher participation from eligible families. When the barrier to applying is a paper form that has to be returned to school, families who are eligible but time-constrained often don't complete the process. When the application is online, available at any hour, completable in minutes, participation rates improve — and every additional properly processed application means additional federal reimbursement for the district.

The FAR-Apps Connection

The online portal is designed as the digital front end to FAR-Apps — not a standalone system. When a family submits online, the application flows directly into FAR-Apps' processing workflow. Determination runs. The result updates COMS-PRO and every cafeteria POS across the district in real time. The family receives notification. The entire cycle that once took days of paper handling now runs in hours — often same-day for completed applications.

For districts that still receive some paper applications, the F&R Scanning Software bridges the gap — OCR-processing paper applications into the same FAR-Apps workflow. The two products together give districts a complete digital intake strategy for any household, regardless of how they prefer to apply.

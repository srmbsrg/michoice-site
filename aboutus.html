Who We Are

MiChoice Technology Systems, Inc. was founded to fill a gap that nobody else was addressing properly: the need for technology solutions purpose-built for the K-12 school food service market.

Other software companies serve school nutrition as a side vertical — one of dozens of industries on their client list. We built MiChoice around the opposite philosophy. School food service isn't a side product for us. It's the whole product. Every line of code, every support call, every implementation plan exists for one reason: to make K-12 child nutrition programs run better.

Our staff brings over 35 years of hands-on school food service technology experience. That's not years of general software experience — it's years in cafeterias, central offices, and district IT departments, building systems that feed children and keep food service programs compliant, efficient, and funded.

Our Approach

We focus on solutions, not just products. School food service technology is only cost-effective and productive when it's approached holistically — software, hardware, implementation, training, and ongoing support working together as one system.

At MiChoice, we work with client school districts to create unique solutions that combine best-of-breed software and hardware with disciplined project management and the kind of ongoing help desk support that's genuinely unequaled in this industry. The result is a deep, collaborative partnership that translates into real return on investment — not just a software license.
